Works with anything

LayerTalk does not import your slides. It only lays a transparent layer over the screen, so it works the same whether PowerPoint, Keynote, or a browser tab with Canva or Notion is underneath.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a room

    Start a presentation room in the Presenter app.

  2. 2

    Share a QR or code

    Show the QR on your slides or share the 6-character code.

  3. 3

    Bring reactions on screen

    Comments, questions, and stamps appear over your slides.

While presenting, press ⇧⌘L to bring up the controls over your slides.
